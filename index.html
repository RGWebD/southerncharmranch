Welcome to the Ranch

Timber, chandeliers & wide-open Florida sky.

Southern Charm Ranch is a rustic-elegant venue designed for life's most meaningful celebrations. Picture crystal chandeliers glowing beneath hand-built timber trusses, barn doors thrown open to green pasture, and a sunset that paints the whole field gold.

Our packages are designed to offer beautiful celebrations at affordable prices, with many wonderful amenities already included, so you can spend less time coordinating and more time celebrating.

The Spaces

Every corner, made for the moment.

01

The Barn

A soaring open-air timber barn with crystal chandeliers strung down its length and wide doors at each end that frame the pasture beyond. Rustic enough to feel like home, elegant enough for your grandest day.

  • Barn & outdoor event space
  • Tables & chairs included

Bridal & Groom Suites

Plank ceilings, whitewashed walls, a cozy fireplace and vintage tufted settees: a calm, beautiful place to get ready, steal a quiet moment, and take those first photos before the doors open.

  • Bridal Suite & Groom Suite
  • Lounge seating & refreshment area
  • Included with every wedding

The Grounds

Open pasture, white ranch fencing and big Florida skies. The property is yours alone for the day, with on-site parking and restrooms, plus golden-hour light that photographers dream about.

Picture Your Day

From first light to last dance.

With an entire-day rental, the ranch is yours from the first bouquet to the final send-off.

  1. Morning

    Getting ready

    Settle into the Bridal & Groom Suites with your people, your playlist and plenty of room to breathe.

  2. Midday

    Setting the scene

    Tables and chairs are ready and waiting. Bring your vision, or ask about the Decorating Package.

  3. Afternoon

    “I do”

    Walk the aisle beneath crystal chandeliers or open sky, with a place set aside to honor the loved ones who are there in spirit.

  4. Golden Hour

    Dinner & toasts

    Gold chargers catch the last light as the barn fills with laughter, toasts and a few happy tears.

  5. Evening

    Candlelight & dancing

    Dance beneath the chandeliers until the moon rises over the ranch, then gather by the fire pit for one last story.

Good to Know

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Call (904) 796‑5354 or email southerncharmranchofflorida@gmail.com.

Where is Southern Charm Ranch?

We're at 5411 State Road 21, Keystone Heights, FL 32656. Get directions →

What kinds of events do you host?

Weddings, bridal and baby showers, birthdays and special events, gender reveals, sprinkles and more, plus corporate events.

How much is a wedding?

Wedding packages start at $4,500 in Peak Season (March–May, October–November), $4,000 in Value Season (February, September, December) and $3,500 in Off-Peak Season (January, June–August). Introductory pricing is available for a limited time. Holiday weekends and select dates may be subject to premium pricing.

What's included with a wedding?

The entire day rental of the barn and outdoor event space, Bridal & Groom Suites, tables and chairs, parking, on-site restrooms and exclusive use of the property.

How much is a private event?

Monday–Thursday: $500 (3 hours), $700 (6 hours) or $900 (8 hours). Friday & Sunday: $700, $900 or $1,100. Saturday: $900, $1,100 or $1,300. Weekend packages are available based on venue availability and existing wedding reservations.

Is setup and cleanup time included?

Yes. Every private event package includes one complimentary hour for setup and one complimentary hour for cleanup.

What extras can I add?

Additional event time is $125 per hour and a cleanup service is $200. We also offer a Decorating Package, Fire Pit Package, Yard Games and a Coffee Bar. Ask us for details.

How do I book a tour?

Use our online inquiry & tour scheduler, call (904) 796-5354, or email us.
